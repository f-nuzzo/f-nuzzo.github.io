Method

How it's calculated

Data. Monthly total returns (dividends reinvested) of US-listed ETFs from Yahoo Finance, used as proxies for each asset class: SPY, VTI, IJS, VEU, TLT, IEF, BND, SHY, BIL, GLD, DBC. The sample starts in June 2007 because that is when every ETF has a full monthly history.

Rebalancing. Back to target weights at the end of every December. Between rebalances, weights drift with the market.

Euros. In EUR mode each monthly return is converted at the EUR/USD rate, so currency moves are included. Unhedged, as a European buying these ETFs would actually experience them.

Risk metrics. Volatility and Sharpe are annualised from monthly returns. In USD the risk-free rate is the T-bill ETF (BIL). In EUR it is set to zero, which slightly flatters every portfolio by the same amount. Max drawdown is measured on month-end values, so intra-month lows are deeper.

Limits. No fees, taxes or trading costs. ETF proxies are simplified versions of the original portfolios (the All Weather here is the widely published retail version, not Bridgewater's fund). About 19 years is one market history, not a forecast.
