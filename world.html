      Method

      Sources and notes

      Data. Each country is measured through its main US-listed country ETF (mostly iShares MSCI funds, SPY for the United States), with dividends reinvested. Prices from Yahoo Finance.

      Periods. YTD and 1 year are total returns. 3 and 5 years are annualised, so they compare with each other on a per-year basis.

      Euros. EUR returns convert the dollar result at the EUR/USD rate. The ETFs already carry each local currency's move against the dollar, so the euro view is what an unhedged European investor would have seen.

      Limits. An ETF is a proxy, not the national index itself: fees, sampling and sector weights differ. Grey countries have no liquid country ETF in the sample. Map shapes: Natural Earth via world-atlas.
