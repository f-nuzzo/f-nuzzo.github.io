Coursework

Selected projects

Mostly group projects from my master's. What they asked, and what we found.

Data Analytics for Finance

Optimised portfolios vs simple 1/N

Built minimum-variance, maximum-Sharpe and equal-weight portfolios from 10 ETFs across 5 asset classes, then tested them out of sample. Equal weight came out ahead, with a Sharpe of 1.24 against 0.64 for the "optimal" portfolio: estimation errors in the inputs hurt the optimisers. We also backtested the VaR models with Kupiec and Christoffersen tests.

The global network of bank lending

From half a million syndicated-loan records (8,000+ banks, 112 countries) we mapped who lends with whom. A logit model showed strong home bias: banks from the same country were 38% more likely to partner.
