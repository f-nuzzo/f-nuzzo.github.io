Method

Sources and notes

Sources. ECB Data Portal for the deposit facility rate, EUR/USD, unemployment, the AAA yield curve and the 10-year convergence yields by country. Eurostat for HICP inflation (the ECOICOP 2 dataset that replaced the old series in January 2026). All free, public and unmodified.

Frequency. Daily series are shown weekly to keep the page light. Country yields are monthly averages, so the spread is a monthly figure, not today's close.

The reads. The one-liners under each number are written by simple rules (above or below target, rising or falling over a year), not by hand. They describe the data, they don't forecast it.
